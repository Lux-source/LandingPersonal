Things I made
Built for family

The AI recommends, the driver decides

Copilot for a self-employed haulier working with Amazon Relay. It reads offers from email, computes the real profit per km including empty legs and drafts a negotiation reply in Spanish and English.

Architecture on AWS and Azure

How it works

with human approval at every step
  1. Gmail Picks up load offers every few minutes.
  3. Evaluator Three-block cost model, official diesel price and a six-level rating.
  5. Claude Drafts the counter-offer in Spanish and English and adapts strategy to the season.
  7. Flask dashboard Nothing is sent or accepted without human approval.

Why I built it

I built it for a relative who drives a truck. The per-km price the platform shows ignores the empty drive to the pickup and the real cost of the truck, and choosing in a hurry among dozens of offers a day costs money.

What it taught me

  • The most important rule in the system is a constraint: the AI never acts on its own.
  • Keeping the AI client in a single module made it possible to switch providers without touching the rest of the code.
